Meadow Clubhouse: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: Meadow Clubhouse does not collect any information about you or your child. There are no adverts, no tracking and no accounts. Everything your child does in the game stays on your own device.

Who we are

Meadow Clubhouse is made by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] in the United Kingdom. If you have a question about privacy, email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

What the app stores, and where

The app saves these things so your child can carry on where they left off:

  • the first name or nickname typed in for each player (up to 4 players)
  • bones, stars, progress through the levels, and which hats, coats and themes have been chosen
  • the grown-ups' settings, such as the prize list and sound options

All of this is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also remove a player in the Grown-ups area.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, location or contacts.
  • We do not show adverts.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not build profiles.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no links to social media.
  • The app does not use the microphone or the camera.

Bonnie's voice

Bonnie's voice is a set of recordings that come with the app. When a line has no recording, your device's own built-in voice reads it. In both cases nothing is sent over the internet.

Buying the full game

The full game is a one-off purchase made through Apple's App Store. The button is behind a check for grown-ups. Apple handles the payment, so we never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only asks Apple whether the full game has been bought. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

Backups

In the Grown-ups area you can save a backup of the progress as a file or a code. You choose where it goes. It is not sent to us.

The website

The same games can be played at holmeshouse.co.uk. The website saves progress in your browser in the same way and does not use cookies for adverts or tracking. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security. We do not have access to anything that identifies you.

Children's privacy

Meadow Clubhouse is made for children aged about 6 to 8. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. We aim to follow the UK GDPR, the UK Children's Code and the US COPPA rule.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
