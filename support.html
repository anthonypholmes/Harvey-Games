Meadow Clubhouse: Help and Support

Need a hand? Email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

What is free, and what does the full game add?

Bonnie's Walk and the first level of every world are free. The full game is a single purchase that opens every level in Meadow Maths and Meadow Words. There is no subscription and there are no adverts.

How do I unlock the full game?

Tap a level with a padlock, then Grown-ups: unlock. Answer the sum (it is there so children can't buy by accident), then tap Unlock everything.

I bought the full game but it is locked

This can happen on a new iPad or after reinstalling the app. Open Grown-ups, tap Unlock the full game, or restore a purchase, then Restore purchase. It costs nothing. Make sure the iPad is signed in to the Apple Account that bought it, or to a member of the same Family Sharing group.

How do I add another child?

On the Clubhouse page tap Switch player, then New player. Up to 4 children can each have their own name, bones, stars and Bonnie outfit. To remove a player, open Grown-ups and look under Players.

How do the bones and prizes work?

Children earn bones for right answers and for finishing levels. They spend them in Bonnie's Bone Shop on hats, coats and themes, or on real-life prizes that you choose. Edit the prize list in Grown-ups. Claiming a real prize needs a grown-up to hold a button.

Bonnie isn't talking

Check the iPad's volume, then check Sound: on at the bottom of the Clubhouse page, and Bonnie talks under Sound & voice in Grown-ups.

A level is too hard or too easy

Tap Ask Bonnie for help in any question. In Grown-ups you can open all levels, switch the warm-up questions on or off, and choose the mix for Bonnie's Walk.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Once the app is installed, everything works offline. Only buying or restoring the full game needs a connection.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
