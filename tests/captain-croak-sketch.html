🐸 Captain Croak: a first sketch

Our own pirate frog for Captain's Cove, drawn flat like Bonnie. He floats on a lily-pad raft with a twig mast and a leaf sail. He wears a pirate hat with a gold lily badge (no skull) and a red neckerchief. He has three moods: making a conjecture, busted, and proud when his claim holds up.
